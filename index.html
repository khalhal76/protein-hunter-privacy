Protein Hunter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026 · App: Protein Hunter (com.proteinhunter.app)

What we collect and why

Search queries. When you run a hunt, the text of your request and your chosen filters (areas, radius, price, cuisines, pantry ingredients) are sent to our server and processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to generate restaurant or recipe suggestions. Queries are used only to answer your request and for abuse-prevention rate limiting.

Location. If you tap "Add my location", your device location is used to add a search zone. Location is used only for finding nearby results and is sent to our server only as part of the search request. You can always type an area instead — location access is optional. If precise location is unavailable, an approximate location derived from your IP address (via ipapi.co) may be used and is always labeled "(approx.)". We never track location in the background.

Nutrition lookups. Generic dish names (e.g. "chicken shawarma") are sent to the USDA FoodData Central public API to ground macro estimates. No personal data is included.

Place autocomplete. Text you type in the area field is sent to the Photon (OpenStreetMap) geocoding service to suggest places. No personal data is included.

Food photos (Macro Check). If you use the photo feature, the picture you take or choose is sent to our server and analyzed by Anthropic's Claude AI to estimate nutrition. Photos are processed for that single request and are not stored by us. Camera access is used only when you tap the photo button.

Voice search. If you tap the microphone button, speech recognition is performed by your device's speech service (e.g. Google) and only the resulting text reaches the app — we never receive or store audio. Microphone access is used only while you dictate.

Favorites and pantry. Stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive an anonymous identifier and subscription status — never your name, email, or payment details. Purchase history is used solely to unlock Premium features.

Advertising. The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising identifier as described in Google's privacy policy. Premium removes all ads.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, no profiles, no sale of personal data.
  • No background location tracking — location is read only when you tap the location button.

AI-generated content

Restaurant and recipe suggestions are generated by AI and labeled as such in the app. They may be inaccurate — verify opening hours, menus and ingredients (especially for allergies) before relying on them.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic (AI processing) — via our server proxy
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat (subscriptions)
  • Google AdMob (ads in the free version)
  • USDA FoodData Central (nutrition data)
  • Photon / OpenStreetMap (place search)
  • ipapi.co (approximate location fallback)
  • Google Maps / Waze / Talabat (links open in those apps/sites under their own policies)

Data retention and deletion

Favorites and pantry live only on your device — deleting the app removes them. Server-side request logs are kept briefly for abuse prevention and then discarded. To ask about or request deletion of any data, contact us at the address below.
